# JOLU SECURITY website: management questions

This short questionnaire replaces the 47-question pack for the purpose of publishing service pages. Your answers decide which service pages may go live and what they may say. Nothing is published until management confirms it.

**How to answer.** For each question, write one of: **YES**, **NO**, **NOT APPLICABLE**, or **REQUIRES VERIFICATION**, or write the answer in words where asked. "We do not offer this service" is a perfectly good answer: the website will simply not mention it. Where a document is requested, a clear photo or scan is enough.

**Please do not** send passwords, login details or bank information. None are needed.

**What we did not do.** We have not guessed any answer from old marketing material. Search Console, Google Analytics and other marketing tools are not needed to answer these questions and do not affect whether a page can be published.
