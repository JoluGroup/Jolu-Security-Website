## Findings

1. **Nairobi carries most of the keywords.** 9 mapped (8 P1), Nakuru 2 (1 P1), Rongo 4 (0 P1). Nakuru's page therefore depends on unique local content rather than keyword volume.
2. **Two workbook mappings are wrong.** KW-093 'security company in nakuru town' and KW-094 'private security company in migori' are mapped to the Nairobi page. Proposed: Nakuru and Rongo (owner decision L-D3). With the move, Nairobi has 7 (6 P1), Nakuru 3 (2 P1) and Rongo 5 (1 P1). The generated keyword data is not edited in this milestone.
3. **'Near me' keywords (KW-086, KW-087) are on the Nairobi page** but are not about Nairobi and are held as claim-sensitive (L-D4).
4. **Service-plus-city keywords stay on service pages**: 80 name Nairobi, 80 Nakuru and 28 Rongo or Migori. Location pages must not duplicate them.
5. **Office data is thin and partly in conflict.** The site holds a name, a label and one address line per office, and two company-wide phone numbers. It holds no hours, postcode, coordinates, profile URL or per-office phone. The Nakuru address and the second phone number differ in the legacy company-profile PDF (see evidence-and-approvals.md).
6. **Rongo and Migori are one page.** The site's office is named 'Migori' with the address 'Near KENGAS Rongo'; the page is /locations/rongo/ and there is no /locations/migori/.
